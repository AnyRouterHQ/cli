{bin} stats — usage analytics

Usage:
  {bin} stats [summary] [--period 7d|30d|90d|180d|365d]
  {bin} stats usage [--from YYYY-MM-DD] [--to YYYY-MM-DD]
  {bin} stats keys [--days N]
  {bin} stats models [--period this_week|this_month|this_quarter|this_year] [--limit N]
  {bin} stats burn [--range 3m|12m|all]

Reports:
  summary   Your requests, tokens, cost, latency and cache hits (default 30d)
  usage     Your spend and tokens per day (default the last 30 days, up to 90)
  keys      Requests, tokens and spend per API key (default 7 days, up to 90)
  models    Top models across the whole platform, not only yours
  burn      Tokens per day across the whole platform, not only yours

summary, usage and keys read your workspace and need a management key with
read:analytics: ANYROUTER_MANAGEMENT_KEY=ak_… or the account's
management_key. models and burn are public and need no key.

Output: a table on a terminal, tab-separated when piped. --json prints the
API's JSON unchanged, so its shape is the API's own and may gain fields.
A flag that a report does not read, or a value outside its choices, exits 2.

Examples:
  {bin} stats
  {bin} stats usage --from 2026-11-01 --json | jq '.totals'
  {bin} stats models --period this_month --limit 20
